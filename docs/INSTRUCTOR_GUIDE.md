# Instructor and Teaching Assistant Guide

## Suggested 75-minute class sequence

| Time | Activity | Instructor focus |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Case introduction | Explain process boundary, mission, and fictional data notice |
| 10–20 min | Process Explorer | Ask learners to interpret gateways, role lanes, and rework loop |
| 20–35 min | Baseline simulation | Have learners predict the bottleneck before running the model |
| 35–50 min | Intervention experiment | Compare a capacity intervention with a quality intervention |
| 50–65 min | Decision Arena | Complete or discuss selected assessment questions |
| 65–75 min | Debrief | Review trade-offs, assumptions, and model limits |

## Questions for debrief

1. Does the highest utilisation station always explain the longest system delay? What else should be inspected?
2. Why does the nominal packing capacity equal 80 orders per shift, and why does demand at the same level leave no capacity buffer?
3. What happens when the team adds a packer? Does the bottleneck move to quality inspection?
4. Why is reducing the observed rework rate not equivalent to proving that a checklist will cause the same reduction in a real workplace?
5. What additional event-log fields would be required to calibrate this model?
6. Which objective should dominate if time, cost, and quality cannot all be improved at once?

## Proposed rubric for a written recommendation

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Problem framing and use of evidence | 20% |
| BPMN interpretation and structural reasoning | 15% |
| Experiment design and explicit assumptions | 15% |
| Analysis of time, throughput, resources, cost, and quality | 20% |
| Trade-off and risk assessment | 15% |
| Recommendation and limitations | 15% |

## Before using the assessment for grading

- Review the wording and keyed scores in `data/assessment.json`.
- Check whether partial-credit scores fit the course's marking policy.
- Decide whether the test is formative or summative.
- Avoid interpreting the competency profile as a psychometrically validated trait measure.
- Read the student rationales; they are intentionally not automatically scored.
- If the assessment is used in a formal course, provide appropriate accommodations and check keyboard/screen-reader use.
