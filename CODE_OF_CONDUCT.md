# Code of Conduct

Project participants are expected to keep discussion respectful, constructive, and focused on learning and product quality.

Examples of expected behaviour:
- Be considerate and specific in feedback.
- Critique ideas and implementation, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and alternatives.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private student or organisational data.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
